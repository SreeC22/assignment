One rule: no completion claim without a read-after-write check. Failures are owned by the agent, never deflected to the user.
Tradeoff accepted: the verification pass adds one canonical-source fetch per claim and will occasionally hold back a genuinely fresh role (slower, slightly lower recall). In exchange, a recommendation the user acts on stops being silently wrong — in a workflow where every false "verified" costs a wasted application or a missed one.
